HELLO,
MALDIVES!

Hello, Maldives!
The Maldives welcomed me with warm air and endless blue. I slowed down and watched island life around me.

Breakfast, island style.
Breakfast was mas huni—tuna, coconut and onion—with warm roshi. It was my first taste of the Maldives.

A little island time.
A local ferry, a paddle, and friendly hellos filled my day. Bodu beru drums gave the evening its rhythm.

GUTEN TAG, BERLIN!

Guten Tag, Berlin!
Berlin greeted me with cool air and a cheerful “Guten Tag!” I stepped out to discover its history and everyday life.

A bakery detour...
“One pretzel, please!” The smell of fresh bread led me into a small bakery. A warm pretzel and a café break became part of my journey.

A little lost. A little braver.
I learned the U-Bahn routes and wandered past the Reichstag. Getting a little lost helped me become a little braver.
